     - Converts all mixed `signup_date` (`303` rows) and `birth_date` (`174` rows) formats (`YYYY/MM/DD`, `DD/MM/YYYY`, `MM-DD-YYYY`) into canonical ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`.
     - Nullifies the 8 impossible `birth_date` values (`1899-01-01` and future `2031-*` dates).
     - Permanently reorders and deduplicates duplicate `customer_id` rows (`1,241 -> 1,233` unique customer IDs).

## One Sentence: Which Governance Guard Was Missing at ShopFast?

> **ShopFast was missing an automated data contract and pre-ingestion quality gate enforcing semantic value-range and distribution assertions (min/max EUR bounds and batch mean/median checks) between the payments producer and the landing zone, allowing a schema-compatible unit change from euros to cents (100× inflation) to silently pass structural checks.**
